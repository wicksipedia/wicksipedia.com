      - Clean Architecture is a tool, not a religion. Bring the right one to the job.
      - DevOps isn't a team or a job title. It's how engineering is supposed to work.
      - The first version of a pipeline is ugly. The second one is ugly too. The third one is where it starts earning its keep.
